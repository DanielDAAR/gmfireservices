Firefighter
gear

We supply complete structural firefighting PPE from the most trusted brands in the industry: Globe and Fire-Dex — engineered to protect when it matters most.

01
Helmet

Advanced impact-resistant helmet constructed with high-temperature composite materials. Features integrated face shield with anti-fog and anti-scratch coating, customizable identification panels, and rear adjustment system for secure fit under extreme thermal conditions.

02
Turnout Coat

NFPA 1971 certified structural firefighting coat featuring a three-layer protective system: outer shell of Nomex® / Kevlar® blend for flame and abrasion resistance, moisture barrier with bloodborne pathogen protection, and thermal liner with quilted insulation. Includes 3M Scotchlite™ reflective trim and drag rescue device.

NomexKevlarNFPA 1971
03
Turnout Pants

Matching NFPA 1971 certified bunker pants with identical three-layer construction to the coat. Reinforced knee and seat panels with articulated design for enhanced mobility. Heavy-duty suspenders with quick-release buckles and adjustable waistband ensure secure fit during active emergency operations.

04
Boots

NFPA 1971 and ASTM F2413 compliant structural firefighting boots. Full-grain leather construction with waterproof breathable membrane, steel or composite safety toe, puncture-resistant midsole, and slip-resistant Vibram® outsole. Metatarsal guard and shin protection provide comprehensive lower extremity defense.

05
Gloves

NFPA 1971 certified structural firefighting gloves with multi-layer design: outer shell of cowhide or goatskin leather with flame-resistant treatment, moisture barrier, and thermal liner. Pre-curved fingers with reinforced palm and knuckle protection maintain dexterity for tool manipulation while providing thermal, cut, and chemical resistance.

06
Fire Hood

NFPA 1971 certified protective hood manufactured from PBI® / Kevlar® knit fabric. Provides 360-degree coverage for neck, ears, and face interface. Worn under the helmet to eliminate thermal exposure gaps and protect against steam burns, flashover conditions, and particulate contamination.

07
SCBA

NFPA 1981 compliant open-circuit self-contained breathing apparatus. Features 30-minute or 45-minute carbon composite cylinder with integrated pressure reducer, first-stage regulator, and heads-up display (HUD) for air monitoring. Provides clean, breathable air in IDLH (Immediately Dangerous to Life or Health) environments.

NFPA 198130/45 min
08
Face Mask

NFPA 1981 certified full-facepiece respirator with panoramic polycarbonate lens providing wide field of vision. Five-point Kevlar® head harness with quick-fit adjustment. Features speech diaphragm for communication clarity, exhalation valve, and second-stage regulator connection compatible with all major SCBA systems.

09
PASS System

NFPA 1982 compliant personal alert safety system with motion-sensing technology. Automatically emits 95+ dB audible alarm and high-visibility LED strobe if the firefighter remains motionless for 30 seconds. Essential for rapid location and rescue of downed, trapped, or incapacitated personnel in zero-visibility environments.

NFPA 198295+ dB
10
Belt / Harness

NFPA 1983 compliant rescue belt and Class III harness system constructed from high-strength nylon webbing with forged aluminum alloy hardware. Features tool attachment points, carabiner connections for rope rescue, and load-rated D-rings. Essential for confined space operations, high-angle rescue, and fall protection.
